# Sancti/Urbis hagiographies ported into Continua (2026-10-14)

Each is a saint file plus a Matins Nocturn 2 reading (the Urbis vita, three lessons combined). The 37 popes are Simplex and all are wired onto their Urbis date (the 10 that tie with an existing Simplex are commemorated under it). Gregory the Illuminator and Philumena are Semiduplex and wired too. Date is the Urbis filename date; "occupied" means another saint already sits on that day in sanctoral-calendar.yml.

## Left out
- Sermon lessons (Augustine for Anterus, Cyprian for Lucius I, Maximus for Eutychian, Ambrose for Philumena and Eusebius): not hagiography.
- Celestine I: lesson 4 is cut off in the source.
- Alexius, Eleutherius, Evaristus, Alexander I, Anacletus, Sixtus II: the app already has a lesson for each.
- Gregory X: the Urbis file is a copy of Gregory II.
- Source cut off (incipits only): Vitalian, Felix IV, Hadrian III, Leo IV, Symmachus, Fridolin, Rosalia, Leonard of Port Maurice, Philip of Jesus, Mary of Egypt, Emygdius, Gaspar del Bufalo.
